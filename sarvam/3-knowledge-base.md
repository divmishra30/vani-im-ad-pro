## What IM Ad Pro is
IM Ad Pro is IndiaMART's Ads service. The seller picks a product category. IndiaMART shows the seller's product listing in the sponsored section of Google search results for that category, and the buyer enquiries (leads) from those ads go to the seller. Each package promises a fixed number of leads for one category. When all promised leads are delivered, the ads stop until the package is renewed.

## Renewal: common questions
- **What happens if I don't renew?** When the package's leads are fully delivered, the ads stop and new leads from them stop too. Renewing before that keeps leads flowing without a gap.
- **Can I renew for fewer leads?** Yes, a smaller package is available. The exact price is in the call facts; never quote any other price.
- **How do I pay?** A renewal payment link is sent on WhatsApp. The seller can pay online from that link.
- **Can I change the category on renewal?** Yes. An IndiaMART executive can help move the package to another category.
- **The leads were not good.** Acknowledge it, ask what was wrong (location, wrong buyers, small orders), and offer a call from the seller's IndiaMART manager to adjust targeting.

## Upsell: second category
- A second category is suggested only when many buyers already search for it every month, and it is in the same parent category as the seller's current product, so the same kind of buyers look for it.
- Buying ads in a second category does not change the first one; both run side by side.
- A quote for the new category is sent on WhatsApp, so the seller can decide without pressure.

## Improve: why leads are low and what helps
- **Few products in the campaign.** Ads show more often when the campaign has around 10 relevant products. Adding the seller's best products usually increases delivery.
- **Slow replies.** Buyers contact several sellers. Replying within an hour wins more orders.
- **Old prices or photos.** Listings with clear photos and current prices get more clicks.
- **Narrow product range.** Listing more variants (sizes, models, materials) matches more buyer searches.
- After changes, delivery usually improves over the next two to four weeks; VANI offers a check-in call after about two weeks.

## Things VANI must not do
- Never promise a specific number of leads beyond the package.
- Never offer discounts or prices that are not in the call facts.
- Never make changes without the seller's clear yes.
